History & heritage

The Estate

“I let the family winemaking gene express itself.”

Born into a family that has worked the land for several generations, I learned very early on the importance of lunar cycles, plants, minerals and observation. An agronomist, and a certified Herbalist since 2020 after three years of study, I have also developed a small distillery of essential oils and hydrosols.

Preserving old vines, replanting family wastelands, creating edible hedgerows… Domaine du Lendemain is a work of heritage, care and transmission.

The Ribière family

The seasons of the estate

2017

First grape samples taken from my father's estate to start future cuvées.

2018

Creation of Domaine du Lendemain. Rescue of 2 ha of very old vines of grey and white Grenache planted in 1900.

2019

3 ha of Carignan, Lledoner pelut and a touch of Muscat petits grains complete the planting. The first cuvées, Jeune Pousse 18 and Petit Bourgeon 19, see the light of day — names inspired by herbal medicine, synonymous with regeneration.

2020

My father Bruno Ribière joins me after 30 years of practice on his own estate — Domaine Ferrer Ribière — sharing the same desire to make free-spirited wines, wines that reflect who we are.

2024

My brother, Vincent, joins the project to create a dynamic setting between nature and culture, drawing on his agroecology knowledge gained in Ecuador. He sets up a diversified Mediterranean orchard, with food-bearing species adapted to the changing climate.

Today

12 hectares in production, with low yields but rich in personality.

The Aspres

Terroir

Located in Roussillon, 15 km south of Perpignan, the estate lies on the terroir of the Aspres — a landscape where the Pyrenees meet the Mediterranean. Green hills, red and ochre earth: this unique setting shapes wines with character.

The soils here are varied: silty-clay, stony clay, schist, quartz, limestone… The alluvial terraces of the Pyrenees bring freshness and purity.

The tramontane, the region's emblematic wind, cleanses and protects the vines throughout the year. A dry, bright, windy climate — ideal conditions for growing grape varieties typical of Roussillon, trained as bush vines (gobelet) or Royat cordon.

Organic · Biodynamics

Our commitments

The entire estate is farmed organically and biodynamically. Work in the vineyard favours observation and respect for living things.

The art of the vine

  • ·Supporting the vine's naturally organic cycle
  • ·Supplying minerals and trace elements through plant decoctions
  • ·Managing natural grass cover
  • ·Écocep and hoe along the row
  • ·Supporting soil life and structure with composted manure and sprays of herbal teas (willow, nettle, horsetail, fern…)
  • ·Preventive sprays of plant extracts, essential oils and hydrosols
  • ·Sulphur and copper depending on weather conditions

The art of the cellar

  • ·Hand-harvested grapes
  • ·Selected grapes
  • ·Native yeasts
  • ·Natural, gravity-fed winemaking
  • ·Rigorous hygiene, constant sensory monitoring
  • ·No synthetic products added
  • ·Every vintage is unique.

IGP Côtes Catalanes Red

Les Centenaires

Among the flagship grape varieties of the département, Carignan had its hour of glory in the 1960s. It is the quintessential rustic Catalan variety and the one that best withstands the harsh conditions of our Mediterranean climate. It contributed greatly to the reputation of the famous Côtes du Roussillon appellation.
For some thirty years it has been ripped out wholesale, but at Domaine du Lendemain we revere it and give it a place of its own as a single-variety wine. The family heritage is no small matter, since it is a Carignan from a hundred-year-old vineyard that I have the chance to reveal to you.

IGP Côtes Catalanes Red

El Pelut

A forgotten Catalan variety, Lledoner pelut is the hairy cousin of Grenache, though much more rustic. The fuzz on the underside of its leaves lets it capture as much moisture as possible and limit evapotranspiration… an ancient variety for the future!
Lledoner means nettle tree (hackberry) in Catalan, an emblematic Mediterranean tree that shades the surroundings of springs… which are becoming ever rarer.

Aura Libre

An aura is the atmosphere that surrounds a being, a plant, a place… it is what emanates from everything in which energy flows.
Our Syrah grows on a plot chosen by the Templars, who settled here in the 12th century. Born of an alleu, then “a free land answering to no lord and exempt from any feudal duty”, they named it Mas Deu.
That is why I chose the famous dancer Loïe Fuller to represent this Aura Libre cuvée. A woman who freed the codes of dance and of the body to become the origin of contemporary dance!
